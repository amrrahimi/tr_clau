# Research of 2026-11-11: 46 strategies on 28 months of data, and the "proven set"

## Why
The owner asked for a full review, more strategies and a bot that makes money. The earlier studies used Toobit's
own candles; Toobit keeps only the last 3500 candles of a timeframe (5m: 12 days, 1h: 146 days), too little to tell
skill from luck. This study uses Binance USDT-M futures 5m candles (public archive, data.binance.vision) from
2024-07 to 2026-10 for the coins on Toobit's top-70-by-volume and 40-most-volatile lists (92 symbols with data,
stock and metal tokens included, as on Toobit). Slower timeframes are built from the 5m candles.

## Method (backend/scripts/research)
- Signals: the bot's own strategy functions (`app.strategies`), default settings, market-state rule on.
- Trades: the bot's rules. Entry at the next open, taker fee 0.06% in and at the stop, maker 0.02% at the target,
  stop first when one candle touches both, fee rule 5% (stop at least 2.4% away), reward:risk at least 1,
  break-even at 50% on 1h and slower. One trade at a time per strategy and coin. R = result / first risk, after fees.
- Every result is shown for both halves of the period (h1, h2), with the share of positive months and a
  t-value of the monthly sums (trades of one month move together, so the trade count overstates the evidence).
- Portfolio test (`port.py`): 1% risk, at most 6 open trades, one per coin, 6 h coin pause after a stop,
  compounding, 5 random orders of same-time signals. A slot is free only after the candle of the exit has closed
  (the first version freed it at that candle's open: a look-ahead that turned +0.01 R into +0.16 R; fixed).
- Not modelled: funding payments, slippage beyond the gap rule, the brain, the AI approval, limit entries.
  Binance prices stand in for Toobit's. The coin lists are today's lists (survivorship bias, small for a
  long/short system). Read the results as an upper bound.

Other findings:
- Exits: wider targets (3R, 4R, 6R), trailing stops (1-3R behind the best price) and break-even change the
  win rate and the shape, not the average (differences inside the noise). Not built.
- Filters on the family (higher-timeframe trend, Bitcoin trend, ADX, volume, distance from EMA 200, number of
  strategies agreeing): none helps on 1h, 2h and 4h at once. The owner's direction filter (trade only with the
  higher trend, never in a range) does not help this family: on 4h the "range" group is the best one
  (+0.053 against +0.012 with the trend). Only trades against the higher trend are weak on 1h / 2h.
- Walk-forward (pick strategies by the first half, trade the second): picked 4h +0.013, 2h +0.019, 1h -0.008,
  rest -0.007 to -0.024. Choosing by recent results adds little; choosing the family by its idea holds better.

## What this means (honest)
- The edge is small (about +0.03 to +0.10 R per trade) and its t-values are 1 to 2.3: likely real, not certain.
  The sets were chosen on this same data, so the future result will probably be lower than the table.
- Win rate stays near 35-40%. No tested change raises both the win rate and the result. A higher win rate can be
  had with the profit lock (50/25: about 50% wins, same average) but it does not add profit.
- Falls of 30-40% from a peak happened in the test at 1% risk. Half the risk halves both.
- Nothing here is a promise of profit. Keep it on paper for a few hundred trades before any real money.

## Built
- 28 new strategies (46 in total) and 12 indicators; all are in the manual list and the history table.
- Proven set switch (`account["proven"]`, panel: timeframes section, on by default): scans and trades only
  PROVEN_BY_TF (1h: 8 strategies, 4h: 12), ignores the manual list while on, ticks 1h and 4h, ends focus mode;
  the direction filter then refuses only trades against the higher trend / against Bitcoin. Trades carry
  `proven`; the panel compares them with the others.
- Real account: the links between paper and real positions are kept in real-links.json (they were lost when
  the app closed, so a later paper close or stop move never reached the exchange).
- Rolling highest / lowest in one pass (same numbers); a chart scan with the proven set takes about half the
  time of the old 18 strategies.

# Part 2 (same day): a free search and Trend Rider

The owner asked for a second look without the bot's rules, a strategy of the author's own design, and a goal of
2-3% a day. Tested on the same data (65 crypto coins; stock tokens left out unless said):

- Scalping with maker limit orders (`scalp.py`, 5m, last 12 months): buy a dip 1-3 ATR under the last close (sell
  a spike), maker fee in and at the target, taker at the stop / time exit, with and without the trend. All 24
  settings lose in both halves (-0.05% to -0.22% per trade), also the ones that win 60-68% of their trades.
- Cross-sectional (`free.py`, long the best fifth / short the worst fifth): momentum 14-30 days about zero to
  +0.07% a day with halves that disagree and falls of 57-85%; reversal negative even with no fee on 4h.
- Trend portfolio (`free.py`): channel states of several windows, sized by volatility: Sharpe 1.0-1.4 on 4h and
  1d, both halves. The only approach that held up; Trend Rider is its buildable form.

## Trend Rider (`rider.py`, built into the bot)
Every 4h candle each coin gets a trend score: the average of five channel states (windows of 5, 10, 20, 40 and 80
days; +1 after a close at the window's highest close, -1 at the lowest). Score >= 0.6: long, <= -0.6: short, at
the next open. No target. Exit when the score is back at 0 or at the stop, 4 x ATR (14 days) from the entry.
1% of the money at risk per trade, up to 15 trades, gross at most 3x the money.
Costs in the test: 0.08% a side (taker fee + slippage) and funding 0.01% per 8 h against longs.

| setting | Sharpe (h1 / h2) | a year | deepest fall | average day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as built (1% risk, 15 trades) | 1.32 (1.21 / 1.44) | +98% | 42% | +0.25% |
| 0.5% risk | 1.23 (1.06 / 1.40) | +61% | 36% | +0.16% |
| top-volume coins only | 1.30 (1.27 / 1.35) | +75% | 45% | +0.19% |
| cost x2 (20 trades) | 1.18 (1.20 / 1.18) | +84% | 45% | +0.24% |
| windows 2-10 days | 1.24 (1.20 / 1.34) | +93% | 37% | +0.26% |
| stop 2 ATR / 8 ATR | 0.87 / 1.27 | +45% / +54% | 58% / 35% | |
| with stock tokens | 1.15 (1.07 / 1.27) | +68% | 48% | +0.19% |
| 1h candles / 1d candles | 0.77 / 0.81 | +41% / +26% | 64% / 34% | |

What the averages hide (as built, 546 trades):
- 26% of the trades win; the average win is +4.1 R, the average loss -0.77 R.
- All of the profit comes from a handful of trades: AKE +155 R, ZEC +88 R, XRP +34 R, BNB +17 R, DOGE +16 R.
  The sum is +284 R; without the best 3 it is +7 R, without the best 10 it is -65 R.
- 13 of 28 months are positive; the median month is 0%; the best +111%, the worst -23%. 46% of days are positive.
- So the 28 months hold only a few independent "events". Sharpe 1.3 here is weak evidence, and the coin list is
  today's (AKE is on it because it rose). Published trend-following results on crypto are in the same range
  (Sharpe about 1 to 1.5), which is the main reason to believe it at all.

The goal of 2-3% a day: no tested method comes near it. The best one averages about 0.2% a day with most months
flat or negative. More leverage does not get there: 3% risk gave +0.35% a day with a 63% fall. A steady 2% a day
would be about 1,000x a year; nothing in this data, or in the published record, supports that.

# Part 3 (same day): the proven set tuned, with a priority order

The owner asked to tune the strategies per timeframe and to give the bot a clear order of choice.

- Tuning (`tune.py`): for each proven strategy on 1h and 4h its main settings and the target (1.5 / 2 / 3 R); the
  best on the first half judged on the second. About half of the "best on the first half" settings were worse on
  the second (overfitting). Kept: only what beat the default in both halves. 1h: bb_breakout mult 3
  (+0.141 / +0.115 against +0.060 / +0.041), target 3R for volume_breakout, momentum, keltner_breakout.
  4h: wide_bar size 3.5, box_breakout box 6, donchian target 3R, keltner_breakout channel 2.5.
  Pattern: larger, rarer breakouts and a further target. Nothing raised the win rate and the result together.
- Priority (`prio.py`, `final.py`): the strategy's place in its timeframe's list (its result over both halves)
  decides between competing signals, instead of the coin's own profit factor.
- Filters tried again and not kept: volatility (ATR above its average) is better per trade on 1h / 2h / 4h in both
  halves, but did not improve the account in the second half; more timeframes (30m, 2h) did not help either.
- More, smaller trades (`prio2.py`): the same 6% total risk over 12 places instead of 6: same result, much
  smaller falls and far less luck. Built as a risk budget: risk per trade = 6% / maximum open trades.

The proven set on 1h + 4h, account test (8 random orders), start 1.00:

| version | whole period (28 months) | fall | second half only | fall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| part 1: defaults, 6 trades x 1%, no order | 1.35 (0.43-2.16) | 58% | 0.90 (0.36-1.36) | 40% |
| + priority order | 3.49 (2.16-6.07) | 57% | 1.35 (0.92-1.70) | 38% |
| + tuning + priority, 6 x 1% | 3.75 (2.59-6.24) | 33% | 1.47 (1.07-2.01) | 25% |
| as built: tuning + priority, 12 x 0.5% | 3.77 (3.10-4.90) | 27% | 1.33 (1.07-1.55) | 19% |
| tuning + priority, 20 x 0.3% | 3.09 (2.47-3.49) | 26% | 1.02 (0.83-1.29) | 24% |

As built: about 34% winning trades, +0.09 R per trade, roughly +4.9% a month over the whole period and +2% a month
in the second half. The tuning and the order were chosen with both halves in view, so these numbers are the
optimistic end; the second half (the last 14 months) is clearly weaker than the first for every version.
(The part-1 row differs from the part-1 table above because of different random orders: with 6 places the
result depends heavily on which of the same-time signals is taken, which is what the priority order fixes.)
